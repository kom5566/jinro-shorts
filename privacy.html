HomeTerms of Service

1. YouTube API Services

jinro-shorts uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you are also bound by the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.

2. Who uses this tool

jinro-shorts is used only by the owner of the YouTube channel @Satowan24, on the owner's own computer. It is not offered to the public.

3. What information is accessed, collected, stored and used

  • Accessed: the channel owner's own YouTube account, only through the OAuth scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload, to upload the owner's own video files and set their thumbnails.
  • Sent to YouTube: the owner's own video file, title, description, tags, thumbnail and privacy/schedule setting.
  • Stored: only an OAuth refresh token, saved on the owner's own computer with owner-only file permission. Nothing is stored on any server.
  • Not collected: no data about other users or viewers, no analytics, no cookies, no device identifiers.

4. Sharing

Information is not shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third party. It is sent only to YouTube (Google) to upload the owner's own videos.

5. Cookies and device information

jinro-shorts does not store, access or collect information from users' devices or browsers, and does not use cookies or similar technologies.

6. Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. When access is revoked, or on request, the stored refresh token is deleted from the owner's computer within 7 days. No other stored data exists.

7. Changes

Any change to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date.
