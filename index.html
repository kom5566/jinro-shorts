What this is

jinro-shorts is a small script that runs only on the channel owner's own computer. It uploads the channel's own finished, original animated Shorts to the channel as private or scheduled videos, together with the title, description and thumbnail. The channel owner reviews every video and publishes it himself in YouTube Studio.

  • Used only by the channel owner. It is not offered to anyone else.
  • YouTube API Services used: videos.insert and thumbnails.set.
  • It does not read, collect or store any other users' data, and it does not collect analytics.

By using this tool you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
